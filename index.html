Med Reminder 1.12 for Android

Never miss a dose again. Med Reminder is a simple, reliable medicine companion — dose reminders, refill alerts, doctor visits and health tracking, all in one clean app.

GET IT ON▶ Google Play

It has everything you need

  • Smart dose reminders — with an advance heads-up before every dose
  • One-tap “Mark as Taken” — your adherence is tracked automatically
  • Refill reminders with low-stock alerts (running low turns red)
  • Flexible schedules — daily times or every-N-days
  • Doctor appointments with clinic, phone number & one-tap calling
  • Blood pressure & blood sugar logging with trend charts
  • Shareable health reports for your doctor or family
  • Private by design — all data stays on your phone, no account needed

Dose Schedules

  • Remind at specific times every day (e.g. 8:00 AM, 1:30 PM, 9:00 PM)
  • Repeat every N days (e.g. every 2 days, every 3 days)
  • Get an advance reminder before each dose (e.g. 1 hour early)
  • Turn notifications on or off per medicine
  • Master switches for dose, advance, refill and doctor reminders

Main Features

  • Clean, easy interface — made for everyday use by anyone
  • Today’s doses at a glance: pending first, taken last
  • Mark a medicine as taken straight from the reminder notification
  • Quick refill flow updates your stock in seconds
  • Save doctors with specialty, clinic and phone number
  • Upcoming & past appointments with one-tap reschedule
  • Day-of reminders so you never miss a checkup
  • Log BP and sugar readings with automatic status indicators
  • Charts show how your numbers change over time
  • “Your Progress” tab: daily streak, adherence %, weekly chart
  • Share a text health report with your doctor in one tap

General

  • Notifications are local — no internet connection required
  • Works fully offline; app doesn’t need to be open for reminders
  • No sign-up, no account — your data never leaves your phone
  • Universal app with full support for phones and tablets
  • English interface
