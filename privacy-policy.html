Your data stays yours

Med Reminder stores your medicines, dose history, appointments and health readings only on your device. We do not collect, transmit or sell any of your personal or health data, and the app works without an account and without an internet connection.

Advertising

The app shows ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device identifiers (such as the advertising ID) and approximate location to show relevant ads, as described in Google’s Privacy Policy. You can reset or opt out of personalized ads in your Android settings (Settings > Google > Ads).

Permissions

The app requests notification and exact-alarm permissions solely to deliver your medicine and appointment reminders on time. These permissions are never used for any other purpose.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this URL.
